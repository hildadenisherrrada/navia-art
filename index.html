The name

Why Navia?

CalleNAVIAMatanzas · Cuba
It's the street where my story began.
Tap the sign

I was born in Matanzas, Cuba, on a street called Navia.

There stands a colonial house that holds the memories of my whole life.

When I started making candles, I knew they had to carry that name. Every piece I sculpt is a little piece of home: warm light, color, and a story worth keeping.

Navia isn't just a name. It's where I come from.

The founder

Hi, I'm Mimi.

Maker behind Navia Art

I make candles that don't look like candles: flowers, fruit, desserts and tiny forests, sculpted one by one in small batches here in Iowa.

I name each piece after people, places and moments from my life. Victoria is inspired by afternoons with my grandmother over a warm cup of coffee. Playa El Tenis carries a little beach from my hometown.

When you light one, you're bringing a piece of that story home.

— Mimi
